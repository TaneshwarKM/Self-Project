# Product Return Policy

## Standard Returns
Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused and in original packaging. Refunds are
processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Defective Items
Defective or damaged items may be returned within 90 days of delivery
for a full refund or replacement, at the customer's choice. No
restocking fee applies to defective returns.

## Non-Returnable Items
Perishable goods, gift cards, and custom/personalized items are not
eligible for return unless defective.

## Return Shipping
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs on standard
returns. Return shipping is free for defective items.
