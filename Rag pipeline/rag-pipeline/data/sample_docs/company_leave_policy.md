# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused leave up to 5
days may be carried over to the following year; any excess is forfeited.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave. Leave must
be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Requesting Leave
Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through
the HR portal, except in cases of medical emergency.
